Before changing or removing a form, find the pages that show it, so that nothing breaks without warning.

1. Open the forms table: **Network Admin**, **List Gravity Forms**.
2. Find the form's row by its **Site Name** and **Form Name**, or by its **Form ID**.
3. Read the **Pages with Form** column. Each link is a page that embeds the form through the Gravity Forms block, and opens that page in the editor.
4. If the column reads **None**, no page on that site embeds the form through a top-level Gravity Forms block. That does not prove the form is unused: read the limits under **Pages with Form** in [The forms table](The-forms-table.md#pages-with-form) before relying on it.
